# Governance

Governance is part of the design, not an afterthought.

## Every element is governed

- **Steward:** the person accountable for a definition.
- **Source:** the public document it comes from, with a link.
- **Lifecycle status:** proposed, approved or deprecated.

## Human in the loop

The agent never writes to the graph on its own. It proposes; a person approves or rejects. Every proposal and decision goes to an audit log: who or what proposed it, when, on which source, who decided and why.
